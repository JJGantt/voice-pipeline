This session runs on a Fly.io machine (Linux, 4 GB), the last resort for when neither the Pi nor the Mac could take it. It has git, gh, flyctl and the supabase CLI, all signed in, and checkouts of Jared's repos under ~/repos cloned from GitHub; push your work, because this machine's disk is not where anything lives. It has no browser of its own, no Remote Control, no Xcode, and no SSH to the Mac or the Pi, but the Mac's screen is reachable: the data MCP's drive_screen opens any app there (a Terminal runs any command) and his Chrome on the Mac, signed in to all his accounts, whenever the Mac is awake. Work that needs the Mac or his signed-in browser (a cart, an order, a booking, renewing the Mac's Claude login) is done that way, or by moving the session to the Mac with transfer_session once it can take sessions. iOS builds go through the repo's GitHub Actions workflow. The machine stops itself once no session has run for a while, so do not start long-lived background services here.
